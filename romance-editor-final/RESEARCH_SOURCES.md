## Narrative Psychology & Emotional Resonance

### Wired for Story by Lisa Cron
**Purpose:** Brain science principles for emotional engagement  
**Links:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Wired-Story-Writers-Science-Sentence/dp/1607742454) | [Official Site](http://wiredforstory.com/wired-for-story)

**Key Principles:**
- Emotions engage the brain faster than plot
- Personal impact drives story meaning (not just dramatic events)
- Triggers dopamine rush that keeps readers engaged
- Stories must answer: "What does this mean to the protagonist?"

**Author Credentials:**
- Former story analyst for Warner Brothers and William Morris Agency
- Instructor at UCLA Extension Writers' Program
- Faculty at School of Visual Arts MFA Program in Visual Narrative

**Additional Resources:**
- [Creative Penn Interview](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2012/08/14/wired-for-story-lisa-cron/)

## Professional Editorial Standards

### The Chicago Guide to Copyediting Fiction by Amy J. Schneider
**Purpose:** Fiction-specific copyediting standards  
**Link:** [University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/C/bo183690033.html)

**Coverage:**
- First fiction-specific guide from University of Chicago Press
- Dialogue, voice, grammar, conscious language
- Tracking character/place/event continuity across manuscripts
- Fiction copyediting tasks distinct from nonfiction

**Additional Resources:**
- [Chicago Manual of Style Fiction Advice](https://cmosshoptalk.com/2022/12/13/advice-for-fiction-writers-and-editors-chicago-style/)

### Developmental Editing Best Practices
**Purpose:** Standards for structural and content editing  
**Link:** [Reedsy Guide](https://reedsy.com/editing/developmental-editing)

**Focus Areas:**
- Plot structure, pacing, character motivation, cause-and-effect
- Romance-specific: meet-cute authenticity, emotional arc supporting romantic tension
- Black moment must genuinely threaten the relationship
- Relationship progression tracking, tension escalation

**Additional Resources:**
- [MasterClass Guide](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/what-is-developmental-editing)
- [Carolina von Kampen Process](https://carolinavonkampen.com/developmental-edit-for-a-novel/)

### Romance Novel Editing Standards
**Purpose:** Genre-specific editorial requirements  
**Link:** [AutomateeD Guide](https://www.automateed.com/romance-novel-editor)

**Key Standards:**
- Relationship progression must be clear and logical
- Tension should escalate, not reset every chapter
- Conflict must be relationship-based (not just external "bad luck")
- Emotional beats must land in the right order

## Genre Conventions & Reader Expectations

### Romance Writers of America (RWA) Standards
**Purpose:** Official genre definition and requirements  
**Link:** [Lucy Monroe Guide](https://www.lucymonroe.com/lucy-at-the-heart/romance-conventions-reader-expectations/)

**Core Requirements:**
1. Central love story (primary plot focus, not subplot)
2. Emotionally satisfying ending (HEA or HFN)
3. Protagonist's internal goal = connection

**Standards:**
- Word count: 50,000-90,000 words
- Something outside romance that characters fix together
- Happy ending is a convention, not optional

**Additional Resources:**
- [Wikipedia Overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romance_Writers_of_America)

**Purpose:** Current market size and reader demographics  
**Link:** [Author Ever After](https://www.authoreverafter.com/romance-market-trends-reader-expectations/)

**Key Statistics:**
- Romance generates **$1.5 billion annually**
- Accounts for nearly **50% of mass-market paperback sales**
- BookTok influences **15% of romance sales**
- Readers demand emotional intelligence and narrative sophistication

**Reader Expectations 2026:**
- Emotional stakes must be earned, not exaggerated
- Genre conventions respected but not rigidly followed
- Readers assume sophistication rather than sentimentality
- Active anti-AI sentiment (readers seek human authorship)

## How This Research Informs the Editor

### Structure Analysis
- **Validates against:** Romancing the Beat (14 beats) + Save the Cat (15 beats)
- **Checks for:** Proper romance arc positioning, genre convention adherence
- **References:** RWA standards for word count and core requirements

### Emotional Resonance
- **Evaluates using:** Wired for Story principles (personal impact, curiosity triggers)
- **Asks:** Lisa Cron's 5 key questions per scene
- **Ensures:** Emotional beats engage readers neurologically

### Copy Editing
- **Follows:** Chicago Guide to Copyediting Fiction standards
- **Checks:** Dialogue, voice consistency, grammar, showing vs telling
- **Tracks:** Character/setting/event continuity

### Developmental Feedback
- **Applies:** Professional developmental editing best practices
- **Romance-specific:** Relationship progression, tension escalation, black moment effectiveness
- **Structural:** Three-act adherence, pacing, plot holes

### Marketability Analysis
- **Informed by:** 2026 market trends and bestseller data
- **Assesses:** Genre alignment, comp titles, commercial hooks
- **Considers:** BookTok influence, romantasy growth, reader preferences
- **Evaluates:** Target audience, cross-genre appeal, series potential

### Character Consistency
- **Standards:** Editorial best practices for tracking character details
- **Validates:** Voice consistency, physical description accuracy, backstory coherence
- **Romance-specific:** Character chemistry, relationship dynamics
